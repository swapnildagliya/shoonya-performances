Frankenstein

A new dance production, freely inspired by Mary Shelley’s novel

Friday · 20:0029 January 2027
Saturday · 20:0030 January 2027

Tickets on sale soon.

Freely inspired by Mary Shelley’s celebrated novel, Glen Lambrecht Ballet’s brand new production, Frankenstein, delves deep into the human soul. Through the power of dance, the performance explores the heartbreaking longing for companionship and love, the creator’s overwhelming guilt, and the devastating weight of rejection.

A compelling theatrical journey that asks: where does monstrosity end, and where does the need to belong begin?
